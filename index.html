MaggieTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

MaggieTV ("the app") is a media player for IPTV playlists that you, the user, supply. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

Data we collect

None. MaggieTV has no user accounts, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics of its own. The developer operates no servers and receives no data from the app.

On phones and tablets, the Google Cast software built into the app sends anonymous usage data to Google, not to the developer. This is why the app's Google Play listing shows "App activity" as collected. See Google Cast below.

If you buy MaggieTV Premium, the payment goes through Google Play, which gives the developer an order record without your name or email address. See In-app purchase below.

Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device only, and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it:

  • Playlist sources you add (M3U URLs, Xtream server address, username and password, or Stalker portal address and MAC address)
  • Your OpenSubtitles username and password, if you sign in
  • Favourites, watch history and playback positions
  • Profiles and app preferences (theme, layout, subtitle settings)
  • Cached channel/programme catalogues and artwork
  • Films and episodes you download to watch offline (phones and tablets, MaggieTV Premium), with their titles and artwork addresses. They are kept in the app's private storage and deleted when you delete the download, clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Whether MaggieTV Premium is unlocked, as last confirmed by Google Play, so the app keeps it when you are offline

MaggieTV opts out of Android backup. None of this data is copied to the backup in your Google account or moved to a new device when you set one up, so after a reset or on a new device you add your playlist again.

Network connections the app makes

To function, the app connects directly from your device to third-party services. Your requests are subject to those services' own privacy policies:

  • Your IPTV provider: the playlist, guide data, streams and the films and episodes you download are fetched from the provider whose details you configured. The developer has no relationship with, and no visibility into, that provider.
  • OpenSubtitles (optional): if you sign in to OpenSubtitles inside the app, your OpenSubtitles credentials and subtitle searches (title names) are sent to opensubtitles.com only.
  • Wikidata / Wikimedia: title names are looked up to fetch ratings, cast and artwork metadata. No personal data is included in these lookups.
  • Google Cast (phones and tablets): see Google Cast below.
  • Google Play: the app asks the Google Play Store on your device whether you own MaggieTV Premium, and opens Google Play's purchase screen when you buy it. See In-app purchase below.

Google Cast (phones and tablets)

MaggieTV includes Google's Cast SDK so you can send movies and episodes to a Chromecast or another Google Cast device. On phones and tablets with Google Play services, the SDK starts with the app, even if you never cast. It is not started on Android TV.

  • What Google receives: usage data about Cast device discovery and casting sessions, with general information about your phone and about the app. Google states that these logs are anonymous and contain no identifier that can be traced back to you.
  • Why: Google uses this data, in aggregate, to monitor and improve the Cast SDK and to detect defects. It is not used to model your behaviour.
  • Security and retention: the data is encrypted in transit, kept for a short period only and not shared with third parties.
  • Your choices: the data goes to Google, not to the developer. Neither you nor the developer can turn this collection off or ask for the data to be deleted.
  • When you cast: the app sends the stream address, the title and its artwork to the Cast device you pick on your local network, which then plays the stream directly from your IPTV provider. For Xtream playlists, the stream address contains your provider username and password.

Google describes this collection on its Cast SDK data disclosure page. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase (MaggieTV Premium)

MaggieTV is free. MaggieTV Premium is an optional one-time purchase, sold through Google Play's billing system.

  • Payment: Google Play handles the whole payment. Your card or other payment details go to Google only, and the app and the developer never see them.
  • Checking your purchase: when the app starts and each time you come back to it, it asks the Google Play Store on your device whether your Google account owns Premium. The answer is kept on your device for offline use. The app sends nothing about your purchase to the developer, who operates no servers.
  • What the developer receives: for each purchase, Google Play gives the developer an order record with the order number, date, price and currency, and the buyer's country, region, city and postcode, used for accounting, tax and refunds. It does not include your name or email address.
  • Refunds: refunds are handled by Google Play. After a refund, Premium is turned off the next time the app checks with Google Play. Your playlists, profiles, downloads and other data on your device are kept, and downloads stay locked until Premium is back.

Google's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

MaggieTV is intended for adults (18+). The developer does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Your responsibilities

MaggieTV does not provide any content. You are responsible for the playlists you add and for having the right to access their content in your country.
